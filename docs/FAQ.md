# FAQ
**What is NotzUp?** A capture, organization, scheduling and AI-context application by oyyo.
**Is it for meetings only?** No. Sessions, interviews, classes, calls, notes, brainstorms, recordings and more.
**Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS?** Targeted, but production binaries not released.
**Can it capture any mobile app audio?** No universal capability; OS and app restrictions apply.
**Can it import Zoom/Google Meet/Teams history?** Planned via authorized official APIs and allowed account scope.
**Can it create a ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini consumer chat automatically?** Not generally; copy/export/open handoff is available where feasible; direct model APIs are separate.
**Free storage?** Registered Free users get 50 MB synced text.
**Organization price?** USD 250/month base plus USD 9 per billed seat, 50GB included baseline, optional larger storage tiers.
**Where do I download?** Only verified releases at https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-notzup/releases when available.

## Updated v4 release policy
Registered Free has **50MB** optional cloud content, **26 minutes/session**, **10 confirmed bookings/month** and **2 registered devices**. Plus is **$5.99/month**, 500MB, 30 bookings and 5 devices; Pro is **$9.99/month**, unlimited bookings and 10 devices (2GB online content inclusion is proposed). No public build or checkout is released. AI processing stays exclusively on the user's authorized device. Received summary links require recipient-specific verification and explicit Free account acceptance when the recipient is new.
