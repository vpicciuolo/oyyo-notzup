# OYYO NotzUP 1.0.27.0

Version 1.0.27.0 brings the current NotzUP library and local-intelligence workflow to Windows, Android, macOS and Linux, with platform availability disclosed on the [download page](DOWNLOADS.md).

## Main improvements

- A simpler source workspace where transcripts, translations, visual notes, visible text, summaries, meeting notes and Creative Blueprints remain visible, editable and tied to the original file.
- Structured meeting and call notes with reviewed speaker labels, overview, topics, key points, concepts and explanations, problems, decisions, risks, questions and action items.
- Creative Blueprint for user-owned images and videos: observed ingredients, editable recreation plan, grounded generation prompt, negative prompt, personalization and test variants. Attention and virality explanations remain hypotheses unless real metrics are supplied.
- Context-aware offline translation with faithful and natural-context modes, supported playful English and Italian output, and clearly labelled beta Napoletano and Barese profiles.
- Read-aloud and accessibility support for populated result sections where the platform supplies an offline voice.
- Optional encrypted backup and storage pools controlled by the user's recovery key.
- Personal public booking pages, time-zone-aware availability and authorized calendar connectors.
- Android-only Barcode and QR Inventory for Pro and Organization, including controlled manual or automatic scanning, catalog comparison and CSV, Excel or PDF export.
- Linux GUI packages for AppImage, DEB, RPM and Snap plus a standalone command-line archive.

## Privacy and source boundaries

Supported transcription, OCR, media analysis, search, meeting notes, Creative Blueprint and generated text run on the device. NotzUP has no hidden cloud-inference fallback for captured customer content. Files leave the device only after an explicit online action such as sharing an export, authorizing a connector, starting an online lookup or creating an encrypted backup.

Creative Blueprint accepts files selected by the user. It does not download videos or images from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or other social links.

## Verify before installation

Use only the platform Store, [notzup.com](https://notzup.com/) or an immutable asset from the official [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/vpicciuolo/oyyo-notzup/releases) page. Compare every direct download against the checksum published in the same tagged release and review the disclosed signing status for that platform.
